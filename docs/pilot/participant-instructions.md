# Participant instructions

## Scenario

You are on call for a synthetic service. Request latency began rising shortly after a deployment. Use the tools assigned to your group and determine what evidence supports or challenges a likely explanation. State one defensible hypothesis and what you would check next.

## What to do

1. Begin when the facilitator gives you the incident symptom.
2. Investigate using your usual workflow or the Incident Atlas workspace assigned to you.
3. Call out the first evidence that materially changes your understanding.
4. When ready, state the hypothesis, the evidence sources you relied on, and any uncertainty or missing signal.
5. Describe any friction you encountered. You may stop at any time.

## Data and actions

All records in this exercise are synthetic and isolated from production tools. No customer data or actual service is involved. The deployment notice is a signed synthetic exercise event; no GitHub account is contacted. You do not need to execute or approve a real mitigation. The UI's approval concept, where shown in the public demo, is simulation-only.

The facilitator records task timings and citation quality using an anonymous participant code. Optional quotations will only be recorded with your consent. No adoption or performance conclusion will be drawn from a single session.
